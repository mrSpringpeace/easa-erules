# CS-VLA.303: CS-VLA.303 Factor of safety

**ERules ID:** CS-VLA.303

A factor of safety of 1.5 must be applied to all limit loads unless otherwise specified.

## Subpart A - General

This regulation applies to all very light aeroplanes.
